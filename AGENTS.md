# Meridian App V.001

## Product goal

Meridian is an internal process-intelligence tool. V.001 analyzes synthetic order-fulfillment activity to find delays, rework, and efficiency opportunities.

## Working rules

- Build one complete vertical slice at a time.
- AI is explicitly out of scope for V.001.
- Keep calculations transparent and reproducible.
- Never present synthetic findings as real business findings.
- Every finding must show its evidence and confidence.
- Keep the app portable: no machine-specific paths or services.
- Preserve the pilot specification and acceptance criteria.

## Definition of done

A change is complete when the app runs from a clean folder, the key workflow works end to end, and the README explains how to launch it.
